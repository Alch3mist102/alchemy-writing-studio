Essential Comma & Grammar Rules
1. Oxford (Serial) Comma: Place a comma before and/or in lists of three or more items ("ghosts, ghouls, and shadows").
2. FANBOYS + Complete Sentences: Use a comma before for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so only when joining two independent clauses.
3. Introductory Clauses: Put a comma after an opening dependent phrase ("When the lantern flickered, she froze.").
4. Nonessential Details: Wrap removable descriptions in commas ("The relic, forged in bog-iron, hummed.").
5. Dialogue Tags vs. Action Beats: Use a comma for speech tags ("Run," he whispered.) and a period for action beats ("Run." He drew his blade.).
6. Semicolons (;): Link two closely related complete sentences without a conjunction.
